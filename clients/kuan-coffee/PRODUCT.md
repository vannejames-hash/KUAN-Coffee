# Kuan Coffee: product

## Who it is for

- **Visitors:** people in and passing through Tagum City who find Kuan on Google Maps, Instagram, Facebook or a delivery app, on a phone, deciding in seconds whether to walk in, order ahead or get it delivered. Google reviews show travellers stopping on the road to Mati and Maragusan as well as locals.
- **Buyer:** Kuan's owner, who sees this demo and decides whether to commission the real site.

## What the site does

Puts Kuan's menu with real prices, its hours, its place on Apokon Road and its three ordering paths (order ahead, delivery, walk in) on one fast mobile page, and makes the shop feel like somewhere worth going.

## Thesis

Quiet form. Good coffee. A place to stay. Kuan is a concrete-and-wood room with a geometric mark over the door; the site is built from that mark and that room.

## Principles

1. **The shop's truth first.** Facts come from Kuan's own material (`SOURCES.md`). Unknowns are hidden or marked ("Price pending"), never filled in.
2. **The mark is the system.** K, U, A and N are the site's only shapes: photo windows, menu selector, markers, buttons, map pin, footer.
3. **Ordering is always usable.** Every design change keeps the menu, the order sheet and the delivery and directions paths working on a phone.
4. **Calm over busy.** Few things move; what moves is the mark assembling, a line drawing, a photo shifting in its frame.
5. **Mobile first.** All four menu lists are visible at once on a phone; the order button follows the visitor once the hero scrolls away.

## Constraints

- Ordering is a frontend demo saved to the visitor's device, labelled everywhere it appears.
- Delivery: a foodpanda stand is on the counter, but no foodpanda page is verified; the delivery button opens a labelled note with Call and Message.
- Photographs: only those Kuan posted itself. The interior is owed.
- Espresso prices are owed; those drinks cannot be ordered until they arrive.
