## Menu

Prices in PHP, exactly as printed.

- **Food ("Classic Flavors", "Snacks")**: the shop's own menu graphic, posted by the Kuan Coffee account on Google Maps (Nov 2025), `assets/source/maps-menu-classic-flavors.jpg`. The subtitle "Traditional taste with a modern twist" is theirs.
- **Non-espresso, Tea, Fruit soda, Single espresso shot**: the shop's menu board above the counter (customer photo on Google Maps, Apr 2026). The board shows a hot and an iced cup above the non-espresso prices; read as "hot or iced" for Strawberry, Matcha and Choco Latte (not Signature Hot Cocoa). Confirm.
- **Best sellers** (Spanish Latte, Matcha Latte, Mochaccino, Caramel Macchiato, Strawberry Latte): the shop's lit best-seller board (same photo). Names only, no prices on that board.
- **Espresso list**: the right half of the board is cut off in every public photo. Names were completed from partial letters only where one reading exists: ESPRESS(O), AMERICA(NO), LONG B(LACK), LATTE, CORT(ADO), FLAT (WHITE). Left out because the letters allow more than one reading: "CITRUS…", "TONIC…", "VAN…", "SPA…", "HA…", "C…", "M…". **Every espresso price is pending from the shop**; those drinks show "Price pending" and cannot be added to an order. Client decision, 2026-11-11.
- Matcha Latte and Strawberry Latte (best-seller board) may be the same drinks as Matcha ₱155 and Strawberry ₱150 (non-espresso list). Kept separate, unpriced, until the shop confirms.
- Some board items carry an asterisk; its meaning is not printed in the photos, so it is not shown.
